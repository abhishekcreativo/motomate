# MotoMate: bike maintenance, fuel and mileage tracker for Android

[![Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.motomate)

MotoMate is an Android app for bike owners in India. It tracks fuel, mileage, service reminders and expenses, and works offline. Your data is stored on your device.

**[Install from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.motomate)**

> This is the public home of the MotoMate Android app: releases, feedback and support. The app is closed source. MotoMate is not affiliated with other projects named "motomate", including self-hosted vehicle-maintenance tools.

## Features

- **Fuel log and mileage:** record each refill and see your real mileage over time.
- **Fuel receipt scan:** read fuel receipts with the camera instead of typing them in.
- **Service reminders:** get notifications when a service is due. Reminders survive a phone restart.
- **Expense tracker:** see what your bike costs you for fuel, service and repairs.
- **Compare bikes:** put two bikes side by side.
- **Home-screen widget:** check your bike's status without opening the app.
- **PDF vehicle report:** export a service and expense history, useful when you sell the bike.
- **Backup and restore:** optional backup to your own Google Drive.
- **Phones and tablets:** layouts for both.
- **Units and currency:** settings for different regions.
